Counterfactual Probe Check

Your current normalized joint intent entropy for Bob is $INTENT_ENTROPY, so there is substantial uncertainty about Bob's current task-skill state. Otherwise choose the best available physical action, delegation, or wait without probing. High entropy alone is not a reason to probe.
