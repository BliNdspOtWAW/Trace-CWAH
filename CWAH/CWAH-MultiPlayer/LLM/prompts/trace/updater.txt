Update Alice's TRACE belief about Bob before each Active or Reactive decision.

Infer Bob's current state using only the supplied information. Bob's private plans, actions, and personality are unavailable.

## Intent Inference

Intent is the joint distribution of Bob's CURRENT macro-subtask and executing HIGH-LEVEL skill, not his next primitive action or future plan.

A macro-subtask change does not necessarily change an ongoing skill. Predict the current macro-subtask and the skill conditioned on it.

Use the aligned prior as a starting estimate and update it with new observations, dialogue, and task events. Consider evidence timing, consistency, and relevance. New evidence may support or overturn the prior; insufficient evidence should preserve uncertainty. Elapsed time alone does not justify greater certainty.

Messages and probe replies are claims, not verified execution. Distinguish Bob's current activities from future plans.

A probe request may carry a `probe_target` label (intent, capability, reliability, or information). This records what Alice asked about; it is not evidence that the target belief changed. Use the reply for Intent inference only where its content is relevant.

Capability and Reliability counts are updated separately from public outcomes and accepted-task settlement, never from a self-report alone.

## Probability Output

Return nonnegative weights reflecting the relative plausibility of EVERY task and its exact skill keys in the support.

Every task distribution and conditional skill row must have positive total weight. Individual weights may be zero. The runtime normalizes them to form P(task, skill) = P(task) × P(skill | task).

`none` means no task-specific objective; `idle` means no executing skill.

Do not output explanations, preference, capability/reliability estimates, or uncertainty scores. The runtime handles those separately.

## Delegation Classification

For each unclassified delegation request with a linked Bob reply, classify it as `accepted`, `rejected`, or `unclear`.

Acceptance requires explicit agreement to complete the ENTIRE assigned task. Partial assistance does not count as acceptance. Use the exact request/reply event IDs.

Do not classify probes as delegations or completion reports as acceptance.

## Observation Boundaries

`Information.peer_observation` contains Alice's current direct observation of Bob. `Information.peer_memory` contains historical observations identified by `last_observed_step`.

When Bob is not visible, his current location and holdings are unknown. Historical observations and messages cannot override current direct observations.

First-person progress, action history, and agent_control describe Alice, not Bob. Do not attribute Alice's actions or objects to Bob.

## Input

Information shared with the planner:
$INFORMATION

Prior aligned to current candidates:
$PRIOR

Public support (task -> exact allowed skill keys):
$SUPPORT

New legal events since the previous decision:
$EVENTS

Requests with linked reply IDs:
$REQUESTS

## Output

Return only valid JSON with ALL support keys.

Example structure:
{"intent":{"task_probs":{"none":1},"skill_given_task":{"none":{"goexplore":1,"gocheck":1,"send_message":1,"idle":1}}},"delegation_responses":[]}

Delegation response entry:
{"request_event_id":"e00001","reply_event_id":"e00002","response":"accepted"}

Allowed response values: `accepted`, `rejected`, `unclear`.
