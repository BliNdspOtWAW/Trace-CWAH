TRACE Teammate Belief

TRACE provides a structured, continuously updated representation of your teammate's likely state and behavior. Actively incorporate this information into your coordination strategy rather than relying only on immediate observations and dialogue.

Use TRACE to anticipate your teammate's activities, select complementary physical actions, coordinate exploration, allocate tasks, and decide when communication would be beneficial.

The representation includes:

- **Intent:** A joint distribution over macro-subtasks and current skills, indicating what your teammate is likely working on and doing.
- **Capability:** Estimated task execution ability, informing task allocation.
- **Reliability:** Estimated likelihood of fulfilling accepted assignments, informing delegation and follow-up decisions.
- **Uncertainty:** Intent entropy and capability/reliability parameter uncertainty, informing when additional information may be valuable.

TRACE beliefs are estimates rather than direct observations. Interpret them in light of recent evidence and their uncertainty, while using them as an important input to your decisions.
